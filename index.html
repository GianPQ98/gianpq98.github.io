About

Gian is a performance marketing specialist with 4+ years of experience across agency, in-house and freelance settings. He currently works as a Paid Search Analyst at Publicis Global Delivery (PGD), managing Google Ads, Microsoft Ads and SA360 accounts — and has separately, independently delivered 28 completed Meta and Google Ads engagements for clients across the US, Europe and Australia through Upwork, holding a 100% Job Success score across that history.

He holds a Master's degree in Digital Marketing from the European University of Madrid (UEM) and a Bachelor's degree in Business Management from Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP), with an additional specialization in Neuromarketing from Centrum PUCP. Before moving into paid media, he spent over a year in Transfer Pricing at EY, building an analytical foundation in financial benchmarking and documentation that now shows up in how he reads campaign data and builds client reporting.

Native Spanish speaker with advanced English and Italian and working Portuguese, Gian has studied and worked across Peru, Spain and Colombia, and is used to running campaigns and client relationships across markets and time zones.
